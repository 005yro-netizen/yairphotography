Frequently Asked Questions

  • What does the price include?

    A standard event costs ₪1,300 for up to 3 hours of stills photography, including photo editing. Each additional hour costs ₪300. Kotel events and trips are quoted by scope.

  • Is a deposit required?

    No deposit is required. Contact us to check availability and agree on the booking details.

  • Can I change the date?

    Date changes are free of charge, subject to availability on the new date.

  • Is video included?

    Yair provides stills photography only. You can request a referral to an independent videographer. Booking and payment are handled separately with that provider and are not part of the stills package.

  • Where do you work?

    Jerusalem and Central Israel, with no travel surcharge within the service area. Wedding photography is not currently offered.

  • When and how will I receive the photos?

    Photos are delivered through a Google Drive link available for six months. The delivery date is agreed in advance. Please download and keep your own copy.

  • What does the album include?

    The ₪850 add-on includes a designer, printing, and delivery. Album specifications and delivery timing are agreed before ordering.
