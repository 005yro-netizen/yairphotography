Full Workflow - From First Call to Perfect Album

Initial Consultation 📞

Every event starts with a personal chat. We discuss your expectations, the atmosphere you want to create, and the key moments you don't want to miss.

Event Day - Arrival & Prep 🎯

I arrive 20 minutes before the official start time to scout the location, check the lighting, identify the best angles, and ensure all equipment is fully ready.

View All Steps +

Family Portraits 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

We start when energy is high, clothes are perfect, and the mood is great. I organize everyone naturally and comfortably, capturing both posed and candid moments.

Reception 🎊

Documenting first hugs, warm greetings, and the emerging atmosphere. I am present but unobtrusive, allowing guests to feel natural.

Event Start - Dining 🍽️

Capturing the mood around the tables, the conversations, and the connections. Every table is a small world I aim to document.

Dancing - Peak Energy 💃🕺

I strive not to miss a single beat. My eyes are on the dance floor, capturing pure joy and magical, spontaneous moments.

Candid Moments 📷

Documenting new guests, intimate moments, and the connection between generations. I'm always looking for the story behind the photo.

Final Family Photo 👪

Just before the end, we capture everyone together again with all the energy and joy of a successful event.

Immediately at home, I perform a double backup of all photos. Then, each photo undergoes professional editing: lighting, color, and quality enhancement.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • 💰 Pricing & Packages

    How much does event photography cost?

    Prices vary based on the type of event and duration. For a precise and personalized quote, please contact me and you will receive all the updated details immediately.

    What's included in the package?
    • Professional photography throughout the event
    • 20-minute early arrival
    • Family portraits at the start
    • Full documentation of all stages
    • Professional editing of all photos
    • Organized digital gallery
    • Delivery within 4 days
    Is there a discount for multiple events or early booking?

    Absolutely! If you book more than one event or book several months in advance, there's room for a discount. Let's talk!

  • 📅 Coordination & Booking

    How do I book you?
    1. Call me or send a message through the site
    2. We discuss your event and expectations
    3. We choose the right package together
    4. We sign an agreement and you pay a deposit to save the date
    5. That's it - the date is yours!
    How far in advance should I book?

    The earlier, the better. Peak season dates fill up fast. I recommend booking at least 2-3 months ahead.

    What if I need to change the date?

    I understand things change. If you need a date change, I'll try to accommodate. Please update me as soon as possible.

  • 📸 About the Photography

    What camera do you use?

    Top-tier professional equipment that performs in all lighting conditions, allowing for sharp close-ups, powerful zoom without loss of quality, cinematic portraits, and wide landscapes. I always bring backup gear.

    Do you come alone or with an assistant?

    For most events, I come alone. For large events or if video is requested, I bring a professional videographer.

    How do you know which moments to capture?

    After 7 years, I identify moments before they happen. We also talk in advance about the moments most important to you.

  • ⏱️ Timing & Delivery

    What time do you arrive?

    I arrive 20 minutes before the start to prep gear and check lighting.

    When will I get the photos?

    Within 4 days of the event, you'll receive a link to a full gallery with all edited photos.

    How long does the printed album take?

    After photo selection and design approval, the printing and binding process takes 2-3 weeks.

  • 🎨 Editing & Quality

    What is your editing style?

    Natural and authentic: warm colors, balanced lighting, professional sharpening, without excessive filters.

    Can I request changes to photos?

    Certainly. If a photo needs an adjustment, we'll make it until you're satisfied.

    Do you retouch photos?

    Basic retouching (lighting, colors) is included.

  • 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Portraits

    Why start with family portraits?

    Because everyone is high energy, clothes are clean, the mood is good, and there's time to organize everyone without pressure.

    Can you take specific family combinations?

    Yes, tell me in advance which combinations you want and I'll make sure to capture them all.

  • 🎥 Video Services

    I specialize in stills but work with professional videographers who can be added to the event.

  • 🛡️ Security & Backup

    What if the camera breaks?

    I arrive with backup gear and swap within seconds if needed.

    How do you backup photos?

    Double backup: an external hard drive and a secure cloud, so no moment is ever lost.

  • 🌍 Location & Travel

    I'm based in Jerusalem and travel nationwide. For international events, we'll coordinate travel and accommodation costs.

  • 🎁 Additional Services

    Can I print large photos for framing?

    Yes! I work with a high-quality print house providing prints on paper or canvas.

    Can I buy additional albums?

    Certainly. Duplicate albums for parents or grandparents at a lower price since the design already exists.

  • ☎️ Contact

    How to get in touch?

    The easiest way is phone or WhatsApp. I usually reply within minutes to a few hours.
