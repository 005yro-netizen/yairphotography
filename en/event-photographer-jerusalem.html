Event photography in Jerusalem

I’m Yair Rozenberg, a stills photographer with about five years of experience. I photograph Bar and Bat Mitzvahs, Brit Milah, engagements and family events in Jerusalem and Central Israel.

Planning starts with your date, venue and the moments that matter to you. Explore examples of my work, see what the package includes, and prepare the details for your enquiry.

Planning coverage for your event

Bar and Bat Mitzvahs

Consider family portraits, the ceremony if applicable, and the celebration. Tell me about any changes of venue so we can plan the photography hours.

Brit Milah

Share the ceremony time and the family portraits you would like before or afterwards. We can plan coverage around the parts that matter to you.

Engagements and family events

Tell me who is attending and how the event is organised. A short list of important family photographs can help with planning.

Photography prices

Standard event: ₪1,300

Up to three hours of stills photography by Yair, including photo editing.

  • Additional hour: ₪300.
  • Optional album: ₪850, including a designer, printing and delivery. Album specifications are agreed before booking.
  • Kotel celebrations and trips are quoted according to photography duration and itinerary.
  • No travel surcharge within the service area: Jerusalem and Central Israel.

Wedding photography is not currently offered.

Planning the photography schedule

  1. Event date and venue address.
  2. Ceremony time or start of the programme.
  3. When you would like family portraits.
  4. Whether coverage stays at one venue or continues elsewhere.
  5. Your requested finishing time.

These details help us decide whether the three-hour package fits or additional time is needed. We agree on coverage and details before the event.

Booking and receiving your photos

No deposit is required. Date changes are free, provided the new date is available.

Edited photographs are delivered through a Google Drive link available for six months. Delivery timing is agreed in advance; download and keep your own copy.

Yair provides stills photography only. An independent videographer can be recommended, with booking and payment arranged separately.
